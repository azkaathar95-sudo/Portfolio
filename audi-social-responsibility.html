The pitch

Imagine a world where your drive for sustainability becomes a catalyst for change.

Drive: Empowering Change is a platform that engages customers and employees to tackle global sustainability challenges. Take the wheel, present creative solutions, and unlock rewards as you drive toward a greener future. One electric mile at a time!

01 — The challenge

Vorsprung 2030 needs people, not just technology

“Vorsprung 2030”

Keeping up with its 50-year-old slogan “Vorsprung durch Technik”, progress through technology, Audi plans to discontinue internal combustion engines and move to e-mobility by 2030.

Social responsibility

To get there, the company leans on its employees’ dedication to sustainability and aims to foster connections with customers and society through engagement and education.

02 — Research to problem

Engage, Educate, Empower

Audi focuses on three principles to make a meaningful contribution to society: each already active in a different part of the ecosystem, but not equally.

Stakeholders

Audi is in dialog with its stakeholders since 2020. The goal is to achieve honest assessment from representatives of politics, industry, science and civil society to its process to progressive future.

Suppliers

Audi conducts CO₂ workshops with its suppliers to identify potentials for cutting emissions.

Young generation

Audi has been engaging with young interns, trainees, and Young World ambassadors since 2020 to exchange ideas on ESG topics across VW Group brands.

Locals

Audi updates neighboring residents on site development and regional engagement through dialog events.

Educate — the gap

Education toward customers is the least developed of the three and exactly where resistance to e-mobility lives.

Important interview points
Would you consider an electric vehicle for your next vehicle purchase?
NREL — consumer view on plug-in electric vehicles

The important problem to solve

Strong opinions against electric vehicles from our interviews, alongside NREL’s report on public views in one of Audi’s largest markets, suggest there is a major gap in understanding the technology and its benefits.

Customers need to be educated to make well-informed, responsible investments into the future. Engagement will not only create more awareness but also help the company target the core issues and help customers adapt to the up-coming future of e-mobility.

03 — My definition

Promoting sustainable choices and behaviors through education helps create a more socially responsible society. It encourages people to be mindful of consumption, make eco-friendly choices, and consider the long-term impact of their actions.

04 — Ideas explored

Three directions, one ecosystem

Idea 01

Online Game

Scenario-based games with situational challenges like efficient charging, strategic route planning, regenerative braking, smart scheduling, and the power of community while driving a virtual electric car.

Grounded in the study of gamification in education: by navigating simulated challenges, players learn how their choices affect both their experience and the environment, turning the audience into savvy electric car owners.

Idea 02

Live Event

A healthy live competition of who completes the track most energy-efficiently with the least emissions, instead of who wins the race in the least time.

Curated, monitored events like these make the difference between an electric and a combustion car tangible.

A platform that includes all the previous ideas and much more.

MyStats showcases the car owner’s contribution to global sustainability, and lets them compare their stats with other users in their region, country, or globally.

Centralizing the numbers and topping them with a reward system turns individual effort into an ecosystem of healthy competition.

06 — Final design

Drive: Empowering Change

The flow that carries a participant from registration through to the vote that decides the winning solution.

Step 01 · Register

A two-step sign-up framed by Audi’s own visual language, entering the challenge feels like entering the brand.

Step 02 · Explore

Themed challenges sit side by side with difficulty and SDG tags, so participants find the cause that matches their conviction.

Step 03 · Commit

Reward, difficulty, deadline, and the SDGs addressed are front-loaded, everything that is needed to commit before entering.

Step 04 · Vote

Votes split between Audi and other participants make the outcome a shared judgement, closing the loop the journey opened.
